What is email marketing?

Email is the channel you own. Nobody can change an algorithm and take your list away. Email marketing turns that list into repeat customers with automated flows that run on their own and campaigns that go out on your schedule.

We set up Klaviyo or Mailchimp, authenticate your domain so emails land, build your template and write and design every send. The platform subscription is paid by you; everything else is in the price.
